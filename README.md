## 📖 经藏法语 · Words from the Sutras

> **心如工画师，能画诸世间，五蕴悉从生，无法而不造。**
> *The mind is like a master painter, creating all the worlds. The five aggregates arise from it; there is nothing it does not create.*
> <sub>— 《华严经》 · *Avataṃsaka Sūtra*</sub>

> **色不异空，空不异色；色即是空，空即是色。受想行识，亦复如是。**
> *Form does not differ from emptiness; emptiness does not differ from form. Form itself is emptiness; emptiness itself is form. Feeling, perception, volition, and consciousness are also like this.*
> <sub>— 《般若波罗蜜多心经》 · *Heart Sūtra*</sub>

> **一切有为法，如梦幻泡影，如露亦如电，应作如是观。**
> *All conditioned phenomena are like dreams, illusions, bubbles, and shadows; like dew and like lightning — thus should one contemplate them.*
> <sub>— 《金刚经》 · *Diamond Sūtra*</sub>

> **菩提本无树，明镜亦非台。本来无一物，何处惹尘埃。**
> *Bodhi originally has no tree; the bright mirror also has no stand. Originally there is not a single thing — where can dust collect?*
> <sub>— 《六祖坛经》 · *Platform Sūtra of the Sixth Patriarch*</sub>

> **若能转物，则同如来。身心圆明，不动道场，遍十方界。**
> *If you can turn external objects, then you are the same as the Tathāgata. Body and mind are perfectly bright, unmoved in the bodhimanda, pervading all realms of the ten directions.*
> <sub>— 《楞严经》 · *Śūraṅgama Sūtra*</sub>

> **知幻即离，不作方便；离幻即觉，亦无渐次。**
> *To know illusion is to depart from it — no expedient means are needed. To depart from illusion is awakening — there are no gradual stages.*
> <sub>— 《圆觉经》 · *Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment*</sub>
